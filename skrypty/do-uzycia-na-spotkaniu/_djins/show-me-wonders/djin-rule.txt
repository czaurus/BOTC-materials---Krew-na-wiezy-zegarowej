Djinn rule: Each night*, all players secretly choose in advance to live or die if they were to be chosen by the Al-Hadikhia. Al-Hadikhia choices are not announced.

Clarification: Starting from the second night onwards, at the beginning of each night, all players must secretly indicate to the Storyteller whether they would choose to live or die if selected by the Al-Hadikhia. This can be via private message, private conversation, Organ Grinderesque hidden vote, or other secretive means. If an Al-Hadikhia is in play, that player's choices are not announced, and chosen players abide by their decision from the start of that night instead of deciding after the Al-Hadikhia acts.
