This Teensy script really looks like it shouldn't work. And yet here it is.

If it's a Typhon game, the good team can't afford to let the Vizier confirm the evil team, so they all have to claim to be evil and/or muddy the votes in case the Vizier tries to push one through.

If it's a Lil’ Monsta game, the Vizier will have to pretend it's not, or risk the whole town rising against them while they're vulnerable to execution.

There's no first night info except snake charming and the Vizier announcement, so Day 1 is all about socials and voting patterns. Then Night 2 gives fuzzy mechanical feedback to both teams. The icing on the stress-cake is trying to figure out whether there's a Heretic or a Philo-Heretic or both…
